About

From native apps to applied AI

I design and ship end-to-end mobile and AI systems across Android, iOS, web and backend. My day-to-day is Kotlin and Jetpack Compose on Android, Swift and SwiftUI on iOS, and Python services that put LLMs to work through direct API integrations, retrieval-augmented generation and semantic search.

I like taking a product from nothing to a store listing: architecture, the AI layer, TestFlight, App Store Optimization and the Play Console release. I also lead teams, and at Kushmanda AI I ran a group of four engineers through architecture, planning, code review and mentoring.

I’m passionate about building in public and turning advanced technology into practical, intelligent products that solve real problems.
